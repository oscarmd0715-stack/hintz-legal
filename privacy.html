Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy describes how information may be handled when you use the H!NTZ workflow for making short videos from internet stories. The exact information processed depends on which integrations and services you enable.

Information processed

When connected services are used, the workflow may process story text and links, video scripts, captions, generated audio or visuals, and account authorization tokens or other technical data needed to operate those integrations. Connected platforms may also receive requests and content required to perform actions you initiate, such as uploading a video.

How information is used

Information is used to find or prepare story material, generate and assemble videos, add narration and captions, and upload or publish content when the relevant service permissions allow it. Do not submit passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information as story material.

Third-party services

H!NTZ may rely on third-party services such as TikTok, Reddit, GitHub, AI generation providers, and workflow/integration providers. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Connecting an account may authorize the workflow to access specific information or perform specific actions permitted by the granted scopes. Review permissions carefully and revoke access from the relevant provider if you no longer want the connection used.

Storage and sharing

Data may be handled or stored by the connected providers as needed to operate their services. The workflow's precise retention and deletion behavior depends on the tools and settings in use. H!NTZ does not promise that information is never stored by third-party providers. Information is not intentionally sold by this policy's publisher; however, third-party providers may process data according to their own terms.

Security and retention

Reasonable care should be taken to protect access credentials and generated content. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep client secrets private and rotate them if exposed. Retention periods may depend on the connected provider and its settings.

Your choices and contact

You can stop using the workflow, disconnect integrations, and use the account controls provided by each third-party service to manage access or request deletion where available. For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the H!NTZ maintainer's GitHub profile.

This policy may be updated by publishing a revised version on this page.
